About STACKLY

A firm built by advisors who believed clients deserved better.

2004Founded in New York
Who we are

Independent. Rigorous. Human.

STACKLY was founded on a simple idea: private clients deserved the same caliber of research and advice as the world's largest institutions — without the conflicts of interest that define big banks.

Two decades later, we still answer to only one boss: you.

Our mission

Wealth in service of a life well lived.

Money is a tool. Our job is to make sure it's always working toward the things that matter most to you.

Purpose

Every strategy begins with your definition of a good life — not a benchmark.

Partnership

We stay for decades, not quarters. Our advisors average 14 years with clients.

Clarity

No jargon, no smoke, no surprises. Just clean answers to hard questions.

Our values

Four principles that guide every decision.

  • Fiduciary always. We are legally bound to act in your best interest — and we would anyway.
  • Radical transparency. Every fee, every trade, every incentive — visible.
  • Evidence over ego. Decisions grounded in research, not gut feelings or fashion.
  • Long games. We optimize for decades, not the next earnings call.
