FAQ

Answers to common questions

What is the minimum account size?

Our Essential tier starts at $50,000. Private Wealth engagements begin at $2M in investable assets.

Are you a fiduciary?

Always. We are a Registered Investment Advisor bound to act in your best interest at all times.

How are fees charged?

Flat monthly subscription or an AUM percentage — whichever is better for you. No hidden costs.

Do you take custody of my assets?

No. Your assets are held at an independent custodian (Fidelity or Schwab). We only have trading authority.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. No lock-in, no termination fees, ever.
